# ADR-0046: Research Mode's Last Five Planned Rows Were A Paper's Structure, Not This Product's

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Context

`docs/product/MENU_MAP.md` has carried five names in the Research menu since
the repository was scaffolded: **research question, evidence, findings,
methods, datasets**. `c9b6359`, the scaffold commit, wrote the whole menu as a
single sentence — "Citation metadata, DOI/public scholarly metadata, research
question, evidence, findings, methods, datasets" — and nothing in `specs.md`
or `project/tasks/10-research/` ever elaborated any of them.

Two of those seven have already been resolved rather than built.
[ADR-0044](ADR-0044.md) built Citation Metadata; "DOI Lookup" was deleted,
because finding an identifier and resolving one are different acts and only
the first is available under [ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md). The row would have been
a promise this product cannot keep.

The five that remain are the structure of a research **paper** — IMRaD. This
matters because [ADR-0039](ADR-0039.md) defines research mode as something
else entirely:

> Research mode synthesises data already present in the document or the store;
> it does not fetch anything… insights, named options and recommendations that
> speak to a design, a feature, or a function under consideration.

That is design-research language. A paper's section headings and a synthesis
over your own notes are not obviously the same thing, and building all five as
aggregate queries over `documents` and `tags` would produce five features
nobody asked for. `project/NEXT_SESSION.md` named this as the trap before the
work began: **the estimate made before the question is answered measures the
question, not the work.** Three modes cost four sessions to that mistake.

So the first task was not code. For each of the five: is it a *store* insight
like [ADR-0041](ADR-0041.md)'s four, a *document* reading like Citation
Metadata, or something needing data `bp-storage` does not hold — which makes
it a schema decision and a different size of job?

## Decision

**None of the five survives as a row named for a paper section.** Two became
part of Research Report, one became a document reading under a different name,
and two were dropped.

| Name | Which shape | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Evidence** | A store insight, needing no data `bp-storage` does not hold | **Not a row.** It is the documents each insight is drawn from, named on the insight itself |
| **Methods** | Neither — a claim about the report, not about the store | **Not a row.** The report's closing section, stating every threshold it applied |
| **Datasets** | A store insight, plainly derivable | **Not a row of its own** in the report; the same closing section carries it, and it *is* a row as **What the Store Holds** |
| **Findings** | A store insight — and precisely the four ADR-0041 already ships | **Dropped.** A second name for what Research Report is |
| **Research question** | Needs data nothing holds: a research question is *declared*, and no schema and no sentence says which of a document's questions is *the* one | **Renamed** to **Open Questions**, a document reading |

### Evidence is a suffix, not a row

Research Report said "rust (4 documents)" and never named them. An aggregate
the reader cannot check is asking to be taken on trust, and a claim and what
backs it are one thing to read rather than two rows to cross-reference. Every
themed insight now ends with the documents behind it, capped at three with an
"and N more" tail.

This is cheap because it is lazy: `bulleted_section` calls `evidence_for`
from inside its own `take(REPORT_SECTION_LIMIT)`, so a store with two hundred
tags runs five queries, not two hundred.

### Methods and datasets are the report's closing section

**This is the half of the decision that fixes something.** Research Report
looked for under-connected documents among the five hundred most recently
seen, because `recent_documents(limit)` is the nearest thing `bp-storage` has
to "every document" — and it said nothing about that anywhere the reader could
see it. A store of nine hundred documents got a report drawn from five hundred
of them and looked complete. The rule was inspectable in `research.rs`; it was
not inspectable from the window, which is where the reader is.

`How this was read:` now states the denominator (documents recorded, how many
of them tagged, tags in use, the span in days), how many documents carry no
tag and are therefore invisible to every tag-shaped insight above, each chosen
threshold, and the truncation — but only when the truncation actually bit. A
limit that did not apply must not be reported as though it did.

ADR-0041 committed to "a plain aggregate query with a fixed, inspectable
rule". This is where *inspectable* stops being a property of the source and
becomes a property of the product.

### What the Store Holds is a row, and it is not really a research feature

The same numbers, reachable on their own, for the reader whose question is
"what has this recorded about me?" rather than "what have I been writing
about?". The store is written to on every successful save without anyone being
asked (ADR-0037), so being able to see all of it in one row is the courtesy
Security ▸ Inspect Metadata already extends about a single file, extended to
the one database this product keeps about all of them. It says outright that
the store holds a path, a title and keywords and never the text — which
`bp-storage` has a test named for and no user could otherwise know.

### Open Questions, and why it is not Research Question

The same correction "DOI Lookup" got, in the same menu:

> **A question has a grammar, so it can be recognised locally; whether it is
> the question you are asking is not something the document says.**

A research question in the IMRaD sense is *declared* — stated by the author,
and the work judged against it. Nothing in this product holds one:
`bp-storage` knows a path, a title and tags, and the text never says which of
its questions is the question. Holding one would mean a schema, an authoring
surface, and a decision about what happens to it when the document moves —
a different size of job, and one nobody has asked for.

What *is* available is every question the document asks, which is a synthesis
act under ADR-0039 — reading gathered material and arranging it — and needs
neither a schema nor a model. The reader decides which are still open. The
report says so in as many words, so the row cannot be mistaken for the
stronger claim.

### The rule lives in `bp-semantic`, not in `bp-ui`

`bp_semantic::questions` is layer one of `specs.md` section 10 — deterministic
extraction, every rule explainable to the user — and it is where a decision
about text belongs. The rule, in full:

- a **fenced code block is skipped entirely**, both backtick and tilde. A `?`
  inside one is a language's punctuation — a ternary, Rust's try operator —
  and including them would bury the real questions;
- a question is a run of text ending in `?` beginning after the previous `.`,
  `!` or `?`, and **may cross lines within one paragraph**, because prose
  wrapped by an editor is still one sentence. It is reported at the line it
  *begins* on, which is where a reader would want to be put down;
- leading Markdown decoration is trimmed, however much is stacked;
- a candidate with no letter in it (`???`, `1 + 1 = ?`) asked nothing.

## Consequences

- **`MENU_MAP.md`'s Research section has nothing planned left in it**, and
  `menus::planned_menu` no longer answers to `"Research"`. A menu with nothing
  planned should say nothing rather than list five things it has decided
  against.
- **Phase 13 is complete as a feature**, not deferred. What is left of
  research mode is generated prose rather than arranged material, and ADR-0039
  already named that as [ADR-0033](ADR-0033.md)'s territory — opt-in, network,
  a separate decision layered on top of this one.
- Two new action ids in the Research block: `OPEN_QUESTIONS` (745) and
  `STORE_CONTENTS` (746). 747-749 remain free.
- `bp-semantic` gains `Question` and `questions`, its first addition since
  `statistics`, and stays dependency-free.
- `bp-storage` gains `StoreSummary` and `Store::summary`, the second read
  function added for research mode after `tag_frequency`, and still no schema
  of its own — ADR-0041 holds unamended.
- **`Store::forget_document` was fixed rather than documented around.** Its
  doc comment promised "and with it any tags that were only on it" and the
  code never delivered it: `ON DELETE CASCADE` took the join rows and left the
  `tags` row orphaned. It went unnoticed for two sessions because every
  tag-shaped query in the crate joins `document_tags` and so cannot see an
  orphan — until `summary` wanted to count tags and had to decide which number
  was true. This is the third time this repository has caught a comment
  asserting a property the code does not have.
- Nothing here reaches a network, reads `bp-research`, or reopens D12.

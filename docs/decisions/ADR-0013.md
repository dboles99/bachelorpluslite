# ADR-0013: Brand Boundary

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Context

The product's personality is a retro-futurist consumer appliance -- chunky
industrial forms, status lamps, deadpan product language. That genre sits
adjacent to specific, protected entertainment properties.

## Decision

Use the broad retro-future genre with original assets and original
terminology. Do not copy names, characters, logos, fictional companies,
props, dialogue, distinctive typography, packaging, or uniquely identifying
colour and layout combinations from any existing property.

## Consequences

- Personality terminology is invented for this product (Semantic Filing
  Apparatus, Clipboard Retention Chamber, and so on).
- Standard and Minimal personality modes must exist alongside the themed one,
  so the product stays usable by someone who does not want the bit.

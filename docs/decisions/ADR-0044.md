# ADR-0044: The Research Menu Is What A Citation Crate Can Honestly Do Offline

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-21

## Context

`bp-research` is the last crate in this workspace with no reverse
dependency anywhere in the application — 5,208 lines and 161 tests that no
user can reach. `bp-notebook` and `bp-execution` left that list under
[ADR-0043](ADR-0043.md); this one is what remains of a count the README has
carried for four sessions.

Two decisions bear on it and they point in different directions.
[ADR-0039](ADR-0039.md) and [ADR-0041](ADR-0041.md) define *research mode* as
design synthesis over `bp-storage`, and are explicit that `bp-research`'s
bibliography types are **not** what that is built on. Meanwhile
`docs/product/MENU_MAP.md`'s Research section names "citation metadata, DOI
and scholarly metadata, research question, evidence, findings, methods,
datasets" — a list whose first half is exactly the bibliography subject
ADR-0041 set aside.

That is not a contradiction, and reading it as one is what has kept the menu
empty. **They are two different features that share a menu.** One reads the
*store* and says what the user has been writing about; the other reads the
*document in front of them* and says what it cites. D12 ruled out
bibliography as the foundation of synthesis. It did not rule out bibliography.

## Decision

**The Research menu gains three rows that read the active document through
`bp-research`, and every one of them is something the crate can do without a
network.**

`bp-research`'s own module documentation is the constraint and was written
before this ADR:

> **Everything here is offline, and that is ADR-0006, not a limitation of the
> implementation.** A DOI has a grammar, so it can be recognised locally; it
> has no meaning locally, so it cannot be resolved.

So the rows are named for what happens, not for what a reader of the menu map
might have imagined:

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Citation Metadata** | `Paper::from_text` over the document — title, authors, year, venue, DOI, arXiv id, abstract, as far as they can be read without guessing |
| **Find Identifiers** | `find_identifiers` — every DOI and arXiv id in the document, with `line:column`, its canonical form, and the URL it *would* resolve to |
| **Check Bibliography** | Parse the document as BibTeX — the entry count and their kinds, or the failure with the line and column that caused it |

**"DOI Lookup" is not one of them**, and the rename is the point.
`MENU_MAP.md` names it, and a row called Lookup that never contacts anything
would be a promise the product cannot keep — the same failure mode
`Access::ReadOnlyBySize`'s message exists to avoid. Finding an identifier and
resolving one are different acts, and only the first is available under
ADR-0006. The menu map is amended to say so rather than left to be
rediscovered.

### Reports, not panels

All three open a dialog, like `Scan for Secrets`, `Hash Document`,
`Inspect Metadata` and `Research Report` before them. `Scan for Secrets` gives
the reason in as many words — "the bar elides, and the positions are the part
worth reading" — and identifiers are exactly that shape: a short list where
`line:column` is most of the value.

A results panel with clickable rows would be better for jumping to each hit,
and it is deliberately not built here: it would be a third near-copy of
`results_panel.slint`, and the two that exist already differ only in which
callback they fire. **Whether those three should become one component is a
question worth answering once, not a thing to prejudge by adding a fourth.**

### What stays out

- **Anything that resolves an identifier.** Fetching from Crossref or
  OpenAlex, completing a half-typed citation, checking for retractions,
  downloading a PDF. `bp-research` has no HTTP client and a test whose job is
  to notice if that changes; adding one needs an ADR superseding ADR-0006 and
  a person to write it.
- **Rendering citations in a style.** `bp_research::Style` exists and is
  tested. It belongs to an *insertion* feature — Insert ▸ Citation, which
  `MENU_MAP.md` lists under Insert, not Research — and wiring it here would
  put it in the wrong menu because that is where the crate happened to be.
- **Evidence, findings, methods, datasets.** These are the *synthesis* half,
  and they are more of what ADR-0041 built rather than more of this. ADR-0041
  says its four insight shapes are a starting set; extending them is a
  separate change against a separate data source.

## Consequences

- **No crate in this workspace is unreachable.** That count goes to zero for
  the first time, and the README's standing item goes with it.
- The Research menu stops being one real row and six planned ones. It becomes
  four real rows and the synthesis list.
- `bp-ui` gains `bp-research`. It is the eighteenth path dependency in a
  crate that is already the shell for everything, so it changes nothing about
  the architecture; it is recorded here only because the *reason* for the
  dependency is a decision and not an accident.
- A document that is not a bibliography will fail Check Bibliography with a
  parse error at line 1, and that is the honest answer rather than a greyed
  row: this product has no `Format::Bibtex` to grey against, and inventing
  one to decide whether a menu row is available would be a format added for
  a menu's convenience.

# ADR-0060: Citations Leave, And The Research Menu Keeps Its Name

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Implements:** [ADR-0059](ADR-0059.md) R1
**Supersedes:** [ADR-0044](ADR-0044.md)
**Amends:** [ADR-0039](ADR-0039.md), [ADR-0006](ADR-0006.md)

## Context

[ADR-0059](ADR-0059.md) scoped the reduction to Windows 11 Notepad plus a
note-organising layer, and put `bp-research` first: **5,208 lines, one
dependant, and nothing depending on it.** It is the largest single removal
available for the least risk, which is why it goes before anything entangled.

`bp-research` is scholarly citation infrastructure -- BibTeX and CSL JSON in
and out, DOI and arXiv identifiers, four reference styles, a research profile.
Windows Notepad does not have it and neither does a note-organising layer;
it is the furthest thing from the target in the workspace.

## Decision

**Remove `bp-research`, and with it the three Research menu rows that read the
document rather than the store.**

Deleted: the crate (7 modules, 5,208 lines), `bp-ui`'s `state/citations.rs`,
the action ids `CITATION_METADATA`, `FIND_IDENTIFIERS` and
`CHECK_BIBLIOGRAPHY` (741--743), their three dispatch arms, and their rows in
`every_menu`'s sweep.

**The Research menu is not deleted, and keeps its name.** Three rows remain,
and they are the ones that were never `bp-research`'s:

- **Research Report** -- aggregate reads over `bp-storage`, worded as
  insights ([ADR-0041](ADR-0041.md), [ADR-0046](ADR-0046.md));
- **Open Questions** -- `bp_semantic::questions` over the active document
  (ADR-0046);
- **What the Store Holds** -- `bp_storage::Store::summary` (ADR-0046).

## Consequences

**ADR-0044 is superseded, and it is the reason this removal was cheap.** That
ADR declined to merge citation reading with the store's synthesis, on the
grounds that they answer different questions about different subjects -- one
reads the document in front of you, the other reads what you have written over
time. **Two features sharing a menu and nothing else** meant the seam was a
single `mod` line and a `use`. Had they been merged as "research mode", this
removal would have been a rewrite of the survivor instead of a deletion.

> **A decision not to unify two things is worth as much as a decision to
> unify them, and it is only visible when one of them leaves.** ADR-0044 read
> at the time like a refusal to tidy up. It was the thing that made R1 the
> cheapest item on the list.

**ADR-0006 loses its most-cited enforcement and keeps its meaning.**
`bp-research` carried the test whose job was to fail if a network-capable
setting ever appeared -- the concrete mechanism behind "core editing never
depends on cloud or AI services", and the reason a DOI could be *recognised*
here but never *resolved*. That test goes with the crate.

**The constraint does not become vacuous, and this is the difference from
ADR-0057's case.** ADR-0025's never-auto-run rule was removed because nothing
could run; ADR-0006 still governs, because `bp-storage` and `bp-semantic`
remain and are exactly the kind of thing that could one day want a network.
[ADR-0059](ADR-0059.md) already said ADR-0006 *"survives untouched and matters
more, not less"*. What it has lost is the test that made it checkable, and
**that is a gap to name rather than a change to celebrate**: the rule now
rests on nobody adding an HTTP client, which is the state ADR-0006 existed to
improve on.

**One comment in a kept crate had borrowed its reason from the deleted one --
for the second removal running.** `bp_semantic::questions` explained itself by
citing *"the same split `bp-research` draws between finding a DOI and
resolving one"*. The split is real and the rule is unchanged; the justification
named a crate that no longer exists.

ADR-0057 found exactly this in `bp_semantic::is_fence`, which had said it
recognised both fence characters *"because `bp-notebook` reads both"*. **Twice
in two removals, in the same crate, is a pattern rather than a coincidence:**
`bp-semantic` is a crate of *rules*, and a rule is tempting to justify by
naming the caller that wanted it. The caller is the weakest available reason
-- it is the one thing guaranteed to change. Both comments now state the rule
in terms that survive their callers.

**Phase 13 is `Removed`.** `ROADMAP.md`'s row for research mode joins phase
12's. Its synthesis half survives under a different phase's crates, which the
row says rather than leaving a reader to infer.

## What this does not touch

`bp-storage`, `bp-semantic` and `bp-naming` are untouched, and so is every
surface over them. **The note-organising layer is the point of the reduction,
not a casualty of it** -- ADR-0059 keeps it whole, and this ADR removes
nothing from it.

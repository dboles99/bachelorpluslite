# BachelorPad+ Brand Guide

## Direction

Original retro-futurist consumer appliance.

Desired qualities:

- humorous but functional
- chunky industrial forms
- status lights
- mid-century / 1970s future optimism
- deadpan consumer-product language
- over-engineered solutions to mundane problems
- "text-processing appliance" rather than generic AI app

## Original terminology

Allowed examples:

- Semantic Filing Apparatus
- Automatic Document Identification
- Recall Engine
- Emergency Recovery System
- Cryptographic Containment
- Heavy-Duty Text Intake
- Computational Scratch Apparatus

## IP boundary

Do not copy:

- recognizable characters
- fictional companies
- fictional product names
- logos
- dialogue/catchphrases
- specific props
- distinctive typography
- copied packaging
- uniquely identifying color/layout combinations

The target is a broad retro-future genre, not imitation of a specific show.

Privacy

Privacy

This page collects nothing unless you type an address into one of the two forms and tick the box. The program collects nothing at all, ever.

The program, first, because it is the part most people are asking about

BachelorPad+ Lite makes no network connection. There is no telemetry, no update check, no crash reporting, no AI and no account. It behaves identically on a computer that has never been online, and because the source is public you can confirm that rather than take it on trust. What it writes on your own machine is your choice and is set out in the manual, under Privacy.

Giving an address is entirely voluntary and nothing here is required of you. If you would rather give nobody an address, watch releases on GitHub instead; it is the same information. The two lists are separate, and joining one does not join the other.

Cookies and analytics

There are none, and there is no banner because there is nothing to consent to. This site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, loads no analytics and makes no third-party requests. A Content-Security-Policy of self means your browser would refuse one, and the build fails if anybody adds one, so this is enforced rather than promised.

Who else handles it, and where

Your address is stored in Microsoft Azure Table Storage in Ireland, and passed to Kit (formerly ConvertKit) in the United States, who send the mail and handle unsubscribes. Nobody else receives it. It is not sold, not shared and not used to build a profile of you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is held, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, withdraw consent, or object. The quickest route to deletion is the unsubscribe link in any message, which removes you without anybody reading the request first. Otherwise write to business@prompt-forge.dev. If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

This is a text editor for people doing their own work, and nothing here is aimed at children. No address is knowingly collected from anyone under 16.

Changes to this page

The consent wording is versioned, and records keep the wording that was actually agreed to rather than being rewritten to the newest text. If this page changes materially, anyone on a list is told before it takes effect.
